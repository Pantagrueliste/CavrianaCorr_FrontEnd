<div class="letter-summary">

Cavriana reports a conversation with a Spanish secretary about Cosimo I's relations with Spain, the precedence dispute between Florence and Ferrara at the Imperial court, the Cardinal of Ferrara's displeasure with the French Crown, Catherine de' Medici's continued support for Cosimo, and Montmorency's mission to England to negotiate a marriage between Alençon and Elizabeth I. Contains partially enciphered passages.
