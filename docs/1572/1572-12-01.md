<div class="letter-summary">

Intelligence report on German and English reactions to the St Bartholomew's Day massacres, Elizabeth I's strategic position, the military situation at La Rochelle, the Prince of Orange's forces in Holland, Count Ludwig of Nassau at Cologne, Maldonado's theological mission to Sedan, the siege of Sancerre, the Imperial embassy, and cipher passages on Orsini, the King's relationship with the Queen, and Montmorency.
