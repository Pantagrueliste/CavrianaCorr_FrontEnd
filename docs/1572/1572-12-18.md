<div class="letter-summary">

A long dispatch of four written sides, roughly a third of it in
                numeric cipher with interlinear decipherment in a second hand. The clear passages
                touch on the siege of La Rochelle and its defenders, the Duke of Alba's position
                in the Low Countries, movements in Holland and Zeeland, and the affairs of
                England.
