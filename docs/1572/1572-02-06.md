<div class="letter-summary">

Intelligence report on French affairs: the King's focus on enemies, judicial reform, money sent to Geneva, factional tensions between Alençon, Navarre, Montmorency, and the Queen Mother, military preparations, Huguenot movements in Flanders and western France, and fears of a renewed civil war.
