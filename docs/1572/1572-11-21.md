<div class="letter-summary">

Report of a lengthy conversation with the English ambassador Francis Walsingham concerning Elizabeth I's support for the Grand Duke of Tuscany, the aftermath of the St Bartholomew's Day Massacre, the siege of La Rochelle and Sancerre, the Polish succession prospects for the duc d'Anjou, and marriage negotiations between the duc d'Alençon and Elizabeth I of England.
