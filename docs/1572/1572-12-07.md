<div class="letter-summary">

Intelligence report discussing Spanish diplomatic manoeuvres, the siege of La Rochelle, military operations at Sancerre, papal politics, Mantuan and Ferrarese diplomacy, the Condé remarriage, the papal legate, and Montmorency's political eclipse. Contains extensive cipher passages with interlinear decipherments by a second hand. Postscript on Italian military levies.
