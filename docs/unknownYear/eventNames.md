**Peace of Saint-Germain-en-Laye**
**Paix de Saint-Germain-en-Laye**
The edict ending the third war of religion, granting the Huguenots four places of
            safety. Gregorian 18 August 1570. Its slow and contested execution is the subject of
            most of Cavriana's reporting through the autumn of 1570.Q1067191**Marriage of Charles IX and Elisabeth of Austria**
Celebrated at Mézières. Gregorian 6 December 1570. Cavriana reports the wedding
            preparations through November and the new queen's reception at court in January.**Battle of Lepanto**
**Battaglia di Lepanto**
The Holy League's naval victory over the Ottoman fleet. Gregorian 17 October
            1571.Q165425**Election of Gregory XIII**
The conclave following the death of Pius V, which elected Ugo Boncompagni.
            Gregorian 22–23 May 1572.Q4541853**Marriage of Henri de Navarre and Marguerite de Valois**
The match Cavriana had been reporting since 1570, celebrated in Paris six days
            before the massacre. Gregorian 28 August 1572.**Massacre of Saint Bartholomew's Day**
**Massacre de la Saint-Barthélemy**
The killing of Coligny and the Huguenot leadership in Paris, and afterwards in the
            provinces. Gregorian 3 September 1572.Q163891**Siege of La Rochelle**
The royal army's assault on the Huguenot stronghold, lifted when Henri d'Anjou left
            to take the Polish crown. Gregorian 16 November 1572 to 16 July 1573.Q650082**Siege of Sancerre**
The eight-month siege of the hilltop town, ended by famine. Gregorian 19 November
            1572 to 29 August 1573.Q3486023**Election of Henri d'Anjou as King of Poland**
The first free royal election of the Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth. Gregorian 21
            May 1573.Q3586956**Conspiracy of Saint-Germain**
The plot of the Duke of Alençon and the King of Navarre against Charles IX,
            discovered at Saint-Germain-en-Laye and reported by Cavriana on 6 March 1574. The
            precise span is uncertain; the dates given bracket the discovery.
